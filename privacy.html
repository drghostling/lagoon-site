Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Lagoon does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The only network use is the optional, one-time download of the Whisper speech model (see below), which you confirm first.

Microphone

When you start a recording, Lagoon records audio from your microphone and saves it on your Mac, in the Lagoon folder inside your Documents folder. Lagoon never records unless you start a recording.

Speech recognition

The live transcript uses Apple’s speech recognition. Lagoon asks for on-device recognition whenever the language is available on your Mac, in which case nothing leaves your Mac. If a language is not available on-device, macOS may process that audio with Apple’s servers under Apple’s own privacy terms; Lagoon cannot control that.

Whisper (optional)

If you turn on “Improve with Whisper” and use it, Lagoon downloads an open-source speech model (about 480 to 626 MB depending on the model you choose, from the Hugging Face model hub) after asking you. No audio or text is uploaded; the download only fetches the model. Transcription then runs entirely on your Mac.

Your files

Recordings and transcripts stay in the folder above. You can delete them at any time in Finder. Lagoon keeps a small diagnostic log on your Mac (~/Library/Logs/Lagoon/lagoon.log) with technical events such as “recording started”; it contains no audio and no transcript text.

Children

Lagoon does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions: open an issue at github.com/drghostling/lagoon-site.
