Lagoon press kit

Timers and live transcripts in your Mac’s notch.

About Lagoon

Lagoon is a small Mac app that lives in an island under the notch. One tap starts a 5, 15 or 25 minute timer, and several timers can run at once. Press Control-Option-R anywhere to record: the transcript appears as you speak, in up to five languages, and it keeps up when you switch languages mid-sentence.

Recordings are saved on the Mac as audio and text, in Documents/Lagoon. Lagoon has no account and no analytics. It works with a mouse, a trackpad or a touch display, and supports VoiceOver, Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency.
